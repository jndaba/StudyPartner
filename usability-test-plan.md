**StudySync Usability Testing Plan**

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this usability test is to evaluate the usability, navigation, clarity, and effectiveness of the StudySync mobile application prototype.

StudySync is an adaptive study-planning application designed to help university students manage courses, assignments, deadlines, study sessions, reminders, and academic progress.

The testing will identify usability problems that users experience while interacting with the Balsamiq prototype.

## 2. Testing Objectives

The usability test will evaluate whether users can:

* Log into the application.
* Navigate the main dashboard.
* Add an academic task.
* View task details.
* Understand StudySync's study recommendation.
* Add a recommended study session to the planner.
* View notifications and reminders.
* Check their academic progress.
* View weekly statistics.
* Find and change study preferences.

## 3. Target Users

The target users are university students who regularly manage assignments, courses, examinations, deadlines, and study schedules.

## 4. Participants

The test will involve five representative university students.

Participants will be identified as:

* P1
* P2
* P3
* P4
* P5

No unnecessary personal information will be recorded.

## 5. Testing Method

Participants will interact with the StudySync Balsamiq prototype individually.

Each participant will be given a set of realistic tasks to complete.

The tester will observe:

* Whether the participant completes each task.
* Where the participant experiences difficulty.
* Whether navigation is understandable.
* Whether labels and buttons are clear.
* Whether the adaptive recommendation is understandable.
* Any comments or suggestions made by the participant.

## 6. Testing Procedure

1. Introduce the StudySync prototype to the participant.
2. Explain that the test evaluates the prototype, not the participant.
3. Give the participant the task list.
4. Allow the participant to navigate the prototype independently.
5. Observe their interaction.
6. Record difficulties and comments.
7. Ask for brief feedback after the tasks.
8. Repeat the procedure with five participants.
9. Identify recurring usability issues.
10. Revise the prototype based on the findings.

## 7. Measures

The following measures will be recorded:

* Task completion.
* Difficulty experienced.
* Navigation problems.
* Confusing labels or controls.
* Participant comments.
* Suggestions for improvement.

## 8. Ethical Considerations

Participants will be informed about the purpose of the test.

Participation will be voluntary.

Only participant codes such as P1–P5 will be used in the project documentation.

No passwords, private academic records, or other sensitive personal information will be collected.

## 9. Expected Outcome

The testing should identify areas where the StudySync prototype can be improved before final submission.

The findings will be used to make revisions to the Balsamiq prototype and will be documented in the project's GitHub repository.
