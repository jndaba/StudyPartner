# StudyPartner – Problem Statement

## Problem
University students often manage assignments, deadlines, study sessions, reminders, and academic progress using separate tools such as messaging applications, calendars, notes, and paper planners. This can make it difficult to keep track of academic activities, prioritize work, and prepare early enough for deadlines.

StudyPartner is a mobile study-planning application designed to bring these activities together in one simple interface.

## Proposed Solution
StudyPartner provides a smartphone-friendly interface where students can:
- Manage courses.
- Create and prioritize academic tasks.
- Plan study sessions.
- Receive notifications and reminders.
- Track completed work and weekly progress.
- Receive simple study recommendations based on deadlines and workload.

The goal is to reduce planning effort and help students prepare for academic activities without last-minute cramming.
