# User Journey

1. **Enter the application** – The student opens StudyPartner and logs in or registers.
2. **Understand current workload** – The Dashboard presents courses, upcoming tasks, reminders, and important activity.
3. **Add academic work** – The student creates a task and records its course, deadline, priority, and details.
4. **Plan study time** – The student uses the Study Planner to schedule a study session.
5. **Receive assistance** – The application presents a study recommendation when a deadline or workload requires attention.
6. **Complete work** – The student studies and marks the relevant task as completed.
7. **Review progress** – The student opens Progress and Weekly Statistics to understand completed work and study activity.
8. **Adjust future planning** – The student uses the information to schedule additional study sessions or change preferences.
