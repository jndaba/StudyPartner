# Target Users

## Primary Users
The primary target users are university students who manage multiple courses, assignments, deadlines, and independent study sessions.

## Typical Characteristics
- Smartphone users.
- Enrolled in several courses.
- Regularly receive assignments, tests, and timetable changes.
- Use messaging applications and/or calendars to receive academic information.
- Need to balance academic work with other activities.

## User Needs
- Quick way to record academic tasks.
- Clear visibility of upcoming deadlines and activities.
- Easy scheduling of study sessions.
- Reminders that reduce forgotten tasks.
- Simple progress monitoring.
- Recommendations that help prioritize study time.

## User Pain Points
- Dependence on class-group messages for updates.
- Forgetting or overlooking activities.
- Several deadlines occurring close together.
- Difficulty deciding what to study first.
- Last-minute preparation and cramming.
