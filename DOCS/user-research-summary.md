# User Research Summary

## Research Method
A short exploratory questionnaire was used with five university-student participants. Questions focused on how students receive academic information, plan study time, manage deadlines, use reminders, and respond to personalized study recommendations. Participants are identified as P1–P5.

## Participant Responses

### Participant 1
- Current method: Follows WhatsApp notifications from the class representative.
- Main planning issue: Needs pop-ups for activities such as make-up classes.
- Reminders: Yes, to meet deadlines and prepare adequately.
- Study recommendations: Yes, because they help with adequate preparation and reduce last-minute cramming.
- Most wanted feature: Reminders.

### Participant 2
- Current method: Uses Google Calendar and phone notes to record important academic dates.
- Main planning issue: Finds it difficult to balance deadlines from different courses.
- Reminders: Yes, because they help prevent forgotten assignments.
- Study recommendations: Yes, because they can help identify which subject needs attention.
- Most wanted feature: A study planner.

### Participant 3
- Current method: Checks WhatsApp class groups and writes important dates in a notebook.
- Main planning issue: Smaller assignments can be forgotten when several deadlines occur together.
- Reminders: Yes, especially reminders before deadlines.
- Study recommendations: Yes, because they could help create a consistent study routine.
- Most wanted feature: Deadline notifications.

### Participant 4
- Current method: Uses Google Calendar and phone reminders.
- Main planning issue: Finds it difficult to estimate how much study time to allocate to each course.
- Reminders: Yes, especially for tests and major assignments.
- Study recommendations: Yes, because recommendations could suggest study time before deadlines.
- Most wanted feature: Progress tracking.

### Participant 5
- Current method: Uses WhatsApp messages, notes, and a calendar.
- Main planning issue: Finds it difficult to keep track of multiple courses at the same time.
- Reminders: Yes, particularly for examinations and major assignments.
- Study recommendations: Yes, because they could reduce last-minute studying.
- Most wanted feature: Adaptive study recommendations.

## Key Findings

| Finding | Participants |
|---|---:|
| Use more than one method/tool to manage academic information | 5/5 |
| Consider reminders useful | 5/5 |
| Experience difficulties with deadlines or study planning | 5/5 |
| Interested in study recommendations | 5/5 |
| Want better organization of academic activities | 5/5 |

## Design Implications
The research supports the Dashboard, Notifications, Reminders, Study Planner, Add Task, Task Details, Progress, Weekly Statistics, and adaptive study recommendation features.
