# User Personas

## Persona 1 – Aisha, The Overloaded Student
**Age:** 21  
**Year:** 3rd year  
**Programme:** Computer Science

Aisha takes several technical courses and often has multiple assignments due during the same week. She mainly uses her smartphone and receives academic updates through class messaging groups.

### Goals
- Avoid missing deadlines.
- Know what to study first.
- Prepare early rather than cram.
- See whether she is keeping up with her workload.

### Pain Points
- Several deadlines can occur in the same week.
- Important updates can get buried in group messages.
- She sometimes studies one subject too much while neglecting another.

### Needs
A clear dashboard, reminders, prioritization, and adaptive study recommendations.

## Persona 2 – Daniel, The Inconsistent Planner
**Age:** 19  
**Year:** 2nd year  
**Programme:** Information Technology

Daniel wants to study consistently but often plans sessions only when an examination or deadline is close.

### Goals
- Build a regular study routine.
- Track completed tasks.
- Receive useful reminders.
- Understand weekly study performance.

### Pain Points
- Procrastination.
- Difficulty estimating study time.
- Does not consistently review academic progress.

### Needs
A simple planner, reminders, progress statistics, and easy-to-follow study suggestions.
