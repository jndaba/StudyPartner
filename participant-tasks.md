# StudySync Participant Tasks

## Instructions to Participants

Imagine that you are a university student using StudySync to organize your academic work.

Complete the following tasks using the prototype.

You may explore the interface naturally. If you become unsure, explain what you are trying to find, but do not ask the tester to perform the task for you.

---

## Task 1 — Log In and Find the Dashboard

Log into StudySync and find the main dashboard.

### Goal

Determine whether the login process and main navigation are understandable.

---

## Task 2 — Add an Academic Task

You have a Database Systems assignment due tomorrow.

Create a new task with:

* Task: Database Assignment
* Course: Database Systems
* Type: Assignment
* Due date: Tomorrow
* Estimated study time: 2 hours
* Priority: High
* Reminder: 1 day before

### Goal

Determine whether users can easily create and save an academic task.

---

## Task 3 — View Task Details

After saving the task, open its details.

Find:

* Due date
* Priority
* Estimated study time
* Reminder
* Notes

### Goal

Determine whether important task information is easy to find and understand.

---

## Task 4 — Understand the StudySync Recommendation

Look at the recommendation provided for the Database assignment.

Explain what StudySync recommends you do.

### Goal

Determine whether users understand the purpose of the adaptive study recommendation.

---

## Task 5 — Add the Recommended Study Session

Add the recommended Database study session to your study planner/calendar.

### Goal

Determine whether users can easily move from a recommendation to a scheduled study session.

---

## Task 6 — Check Notifications and Reminders

Find the notifications and reminders related to your Database assignment.

### Goal

Determine whether users can find upcoming deadlines and understand reminder information.

---

## Task 7 — Check Academic Progress

Find your current progress and weekly statistics.

Identify:

* Number of completed tasks
* Number of pending tasks
* Study hours
* Course with the lowest progress

### Goal

Determine whether users can understand their academic progress.

---

## Task 8 — Change Study Preferences

Open Profile and Settings.

Find the preferred study time and weekly study target.

### Goal

Determine whether users can find and understand personalization settings.

---

## Final Feedback

After completing the tasks, answer:

1. What was easiest to use?
2. What was most difficult to use?
3. Was anything confusing?
4. Which feature did you find most useful?
5. What would you change about StudySync?
